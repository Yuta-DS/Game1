The Three Kingdoms

How each world reads at a glance — drawn from the scene files themselves, so the ground tones, light colours, landmark positions and on-screen questions are the ones already in the game. Views look south across each island from just above the treasure.

Illustrations are generated from mind_spark_kingdom.tscn, heart_melody_kingdom.tscn and creators_light_kingdom.tscn — landmark X/Z positions, ground_colour, light_color, fog_light_color and every Label3D string are read from those files rather than invented. They show layout, palette and mood, not final art.
