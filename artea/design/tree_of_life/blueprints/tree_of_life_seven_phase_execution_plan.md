# Tree of Life — Seven Connected Blueprint Gates

Visual authority: `tree_of_life_complete_architecture_xmas_v1.png`.

No phase is complete because its object counts exist. A phase passes only when
its measured production geometry, production-scene captures, attachment checks,
and all earlier phase gates pass together.

## Phase 1 — Front elevation

Build the 64 m total height, 52 m maximum canopy width, 36 m root spread,
48 m trunk/root body, 6 m x 8 m doorway, 14 m heart center, nine-tier cone,
and 4 m crown star as one readable south-facing elevation.

Gate: measured dimensions are within 3%, the doorway and heart are unobstructed,
the crown is dense rather than skeletal, and the production front capture
visually matches the blueprint silhouette.

## Phase 2 — Side elevation

Give the front elevation equivalent depth, old-growth trunk mass, grounded root
flare, centered heart recess, doorway profile, and a full 360-degree canopy.

Gate: front and side projected widths differ by no more than 5%; neither view
reveals a flat card wall, hollow trunk, missing roots, or floating geometry.

## Phase 3 — Top-down whole plan

Arrange nine alternating radial whorls, 12 primary roots, exactly two rootlets
per root, and the protected south doorway/heart corridor.

Gate: the top capture shows complete radial coverage without a vertical branch
fence; measured counts are 12 roots and 24 rootlets; the south corridor is clear.

## Phase 4 — Branch hierarchy

Build dedicated primary, child, and twig modules with explicit root and child
sockets. Assemble 72 primaries, exactly two children per primary, and exactly
six twig fans per child.

Gate: branch-only front, side, top, and socket close-ups pass calculated gap
tolerances of 0.01 m, 0.005 m, and 0.003 m respectively.

## Phase 5 — Leaf system

Place exactly 28,800 visible two-triangle cards on named twig sockets: 10,656
nature, 6,912 magic, 6,912 technology, and 4,320 solar-glass leaves.

Gate: every visible leaf origin coincides with a twig socket, the heart corridor
is refilled elsewhere rather than reducing the count, and all branches receive
continuous foliage coverage in front, side, top, and close-up captures.

## Phase 6 — Trunk/root module

Finish the ancient fused leader, doorway buttresses, co-dominant stems, bark
ridges, knot scars, 12 grounded primary roots, and 24 grounded rootlets.

Gate: trunk/root-only front, side, top, and three-quarter captures match the
blueprint; all roots touch the foundation; the production player crosses both
directions without catching.

## Phase 7 — Energy-flow routing

Connect 24 rootlet intakes to 12 root channels, six buttress collectors, two
heart feeders, 18 trunk veins, 72 bough conduits, 144 child conduits, 864 twig
filaments, leaf regions, and the crown star.

Gate: endpoint distances pass numerically, canopy-hidden and complete-tree
captures show one unbroken routed system with no cyan line crossing open air,
and all seven phase gates plus laptop/Web performance budgets pass together.
