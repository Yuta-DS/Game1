The earlier 55–70 minute World One plan is now shared across Mind Spark, Heart Melody, and
Creator’s Light.

 transitions + 7 minutes for the Castle finale. Exploration, retries, and optional reflection create the 55–70
 minute player range.

Total content budget
        3 worlds, each with 2 short quests and 1 three-stage boss.
        6 normal monster designs, but only 2 shared Godot enemy script templates.
        15 main story tasks: 5 tasks in each world, including the boss.
        6 required game choices: 2 in each world. Personal questions stay optional.
        Levels 1–5 across the complete campaign, not Levels 1–5 inside every world.
        The Crown Railway cannot be ridden before World One is complete.

 PLAYER VALUE RULE The player fights corrupted Blight shells and dangerous machines. Thoughts,
 feelings, creativity, Milo, guardians, and friendly creatures are never treated as enemies.

PART 1

The Complete Player Journey
The three kingdoms are short chapters of one connected story. Every treasure changes the Art
Castle and opens the next step.

Opening — Mayor Milo asks for help
ARTÉA Town • Meet the mayor and walk to the Art Castle

The player arrives with a crystal sword. Mayor Milo explains that Static Blight creatures are draining the town
and the Art Castle has gone dark. He asks for help and leads the player toward the castle. Two weak creatures
teach movement, dodge, block, and sword attack. Milo opens the castle with the town key.

The locked railway and the first gate
Art Castle Main Gallery • Understand the full mission

Inside the Gallery, the player sees three empty treasure displays and the Crown Railway behind a dark seal.
Milo explains that the train has no power and cannot be ridden. The Mind Spark World Gate is the only open
path. The player must recover the first treasure to wake the train, then use the train to reach the other
kingdoms.

Chapter One — Wisdom
Mind Spark Kingdom • Understand one thought and free the Star of Wisdom

The player clears Static Blight from a living computer forest, collects Thought Sparks, reads a simple pattern,
and faces the Thought Storm Warden. The final Hope action frees the Guardian and turns the storm into
gentle rain. The Star of Wisdom opens the return World Gate.

First train ride — The heart route
Art Castle Gallery Station • Wake the railway and travel to World Two

The player places the Star in the Gallery. The dark railway seal breaks, and the train wakes for the first time.
Mayor Milo and the player board together. Heart Melody becomes the first destination. The short ride shows
Mind Spark shining behind them and the green valley ahead.

Chapter Two — Feeling and connection
Heart Melody Kingdom • Give feelings a safe voice and earn the Harmony Jewel

The valley is silent because Discord Blight has trapped the instruments. The player fights the Blight shells,
uses drums, bells, water, and flowers to express different feelings, and helps Milo reconnect the music
machines. The Silent Conductor boss is defeated when the player combines different feeling sounds into one
honest chord.

Second train ride — The creation route
Heart Melody Station • Place the Jewel and open World Three

The Harmony Jewel powers the next railway line. The train plays the player’s short melody during the ride.
Creator’s Light appears as a world of blank frames, floating shapes, glass seeds, and silver pipes waiting for
imagination.

Chapter Three — Creativity
Creator’s Light Kingdom • Make one brave mark and earn the Creator’s Light

Blank Blight creatures erase paths and cover useful objects. The player fights them, uses a small creation
palette to make a bridge, door, tree, or light, and learns that art does not need to be perfect. The Perfect
Blank boss falls when the player uses an unfinished creation as the final answer.

Finale — The Crown of Life
ARTÉA Castle • Join the three treasures and celebrate the player’s light

The train returns to the castle. The Star of Wisdom, Harmony Jewel, and Creator’s Light become the Light of
Identity. Milo offers two or three friendly identity suggestions, but the player may choose another or say, ‘I
am still discovering.’ The Maker shapes the light into the Crown of Life. Free train travel and replay open as
post-game rewards.

Build order
1.    Finish the town, castle entrance, locked railway, and Mind Spark World Gate state.
     2. Build the two shared enemy bases and prove all six enemy variations work with the current sword.
     3. Build one BossBase and test the Mind Spark boss before making the other boss variations.
     4. Finish the full World One loop and first train unlock.
     5. Add Heart Melody using pre-recorded sounds and existing platform systems.
     6. Add Creator’s Light using prebuilt stamps and colour/material choices.
     7. Connect the three treasures, Castle finale, identity choice, Crown of Life, and free travel.
     8. Test the 55–70 minute target, mobile frame rate, saving, checkpoints, combat assist, and reduced motion.

 DO NOT OVERBUILD Do not create a full procedural music engine, AI drawing-to-3D system, six separate
 enemy codebases, or three different train systems for this short campaign.

PART 7

Castle Finale & Acceptance Checklist
The final 5–7 minutes bring the three discoveries together without adding a fourth full world.

Finale sequence
1.    The train returns from Creator’s Light and enters Celebration Mode.
     2. The player places the Creator’s Light beside the Star of Wisdom and Harmony Jewel.
     3. The three treasures become the Light of Identity.
     4. Milo shows two or three friendly identity suggestions based on saved choices.
     5. The player chooses one, selects another, or chooses ‘I am still discovering.’
     6. The Maker creates the Crown of Life and reminds the player that the treasures did not create their value.
     7. A star receives the player’s chosen name or symbol. Free railway travel and world replay open.

 FINAL MESSAGE Your story matters. You are uniquely created. Your light has a place in this universe.



The campaign is ready when
     ☐ A normal first playthrough finishes in 55–70 minutes, including all three worlds and the Castle finale.
     ☐ World One can be entered without riding the train, and the train stays sealed until the Star is placed.
     ☐ Each world contains exactly two short quests, two normal enemy families, and one three-stage boss.
     ☐ All required fights and story rewards give enough XP to reach Level 5 without grinding.
     ☐ The player can finish the story after a defeat without losing XP, treasure, solved puzzles, or completed tasks.
     ☐ Feelings, thoughts, creativity, Milo, guardians, and friendly creatures are never attack targets.
     ☐ Heart Melody uses pre-recorded sounds and Creator’s Light uses prebuilt object stamps, keeping both
       systems practical in Godot.
     ☐ World completion, treasure placement, train routes, levels, XP, checkpoints, and the finale save and load
       correctly.
     ☐ No normal encounter has more than three enemies, and the project meets the mobile/GL Compatibility
       frame-rate target.
     ☐ Subtitles, larger text, symbols, combat assist, reduced motion, checkpoints, optional reflection, and skip work
        correctly.
