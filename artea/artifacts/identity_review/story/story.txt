1. The Main Game Idea

ARTÉA is a story about a young person discovering the light inside them. The player travels
through ARTÉA Town with Milo, a friendly glowing AI helper. Together, they visit three special
kingdoms and return to the ARTÉA Castle for a joyful celebration.

What the world looks like

The whole game mixes three ideas.

  • Nature brings trees, flowers, water, weather, animals, and growth.

  • Magic brings glowing light, treasures, guardians, stars, and wonder.

  • Technology brings Milo, computer paths, musical machines, and art that becomes real.

The journey

  • First, the player learns to understand their thoughts.

  • Next, the player learns to express their feelings.

  • Then, the player uses art to show their creativity.

  • Finally, the player discovers their strengths, identity, and purpose.

The places already inside ARTÉA

ARTÉA Town: The safe centre of the game. The player can explore, meet Milo, and choose
where to go next.
Three game buildings: Each building is a doorway to one of the three kingdoms.
ARTÉA Gallery: The place where the player can see their thoughts, music, art, memories, and
treasures.
ARTÉA Castle: The final place where every discovery comes together.

How the story begins

ARTÉA Town has become quiet. The castle still stands, but its light is sleeping. The paths
between the Mind, Heart, and Creative worlds are no longer connected.

When the player arrives, Milo wakes up. He tells the player that the castle is not broken. It is
waiting for a new story and a new light.

2. The Connected Crown of Life Journey

The three mini games are not separate activities. They are three chapters of one connected
adventure. Every world helps the player discover something beautiful that is already growing
inside them.

The castle and Gallery universe

The ARTÉA Castle is the centre of the whole journey. Inside the castle is a living universe filled
with stars, music, colour, memories, and artwork. The ARTÉA Gallery is the place where every
discovery becomes part of the player’s personal story.

After each kingdom, the player returns to the town or Gallery. They can see that their new
treasure has changed the castle. This makes every world feel connected to the same
adventure.

The player is discovering, not repairing

The player is not collecting broken pieces, because the player is not broken. They are
discovering inner treasures that were already waiting to be seen.

  • Inner strengths

  • Special gifts

  • Lights of hope

  • Jewels of identity

  • Creative possibilities

The three treasures

The Star of Wisdom: Found in Mind Spark Kingdom. It shows wisdom, courage, hope, and the
strength to understand thoughts.
The Harmony Jewel: Found in Heart Melody Kingdom. It shows emotional awareness,
kindness, connection, and the courage to express feelings.
The Creator’s Light: Found in Creator’s Light Kingdom. It shows imagination, creativity,
identity, and the power to share a unique gift.

The Rainbow Galaxy Train

The Rainbow Galaxy Train is the main way to travel through the castle universe. Its home is the
ARTÉA Gallery Station. The railway connects the Gallery, Mind Spark Kingdom, Heart Melody
Kingdom, and Creator’s Light Kingdom.

The train is not only a travel menu. The player rides inside it, controls it, looks out of the
windows, and watches the worlds change as the journey grows.

The main Crown Line

  MAIN ROUTE ARTÉA Gallery → Mind Spark Kingdom → Heart Melody Kingdom →
  Creator’s Light Kingdom → ARTÉA Gallery

During the first story journey, the train follows this main route in order. The next world opens
only after the player completes the current world and brings its treasure back to the Gallery.

How each station unlocks

At the beginning: Only ARTÉA Gallery Station and Mind Spark Station are open.
After World One: Mind Spark Station stays open. The player can return to it, and Heart Melody
Station becomes the next story stop.
After World Two: Heart Melody Station stays open. Creator’s Light Station becomes the next
story stop, and the shortcut controls wake up.
After World Three: Every station and every route is open. The player can travel freely and
enjoy the complete galaxy railway.

The Star Shortcut Lines

The shortcut railway gives the player freedom after a world has been unlocked. At any open
station, the player enters the train cabin and turns the Destination Wheel towards the world they
want to visit.

  SHORTCUT ROUTES ARTÉA Gallery ↔ Any unlocked world                       Any unlocked world ↔
  Another unlocked world

When the player chooses a shortcut, glowing rails grow across the galaxy and connect to the
chosen station. The journey is faster than the main route, but it is still a real train ride. The
player experiences the movement and scenery instead of jumping instantly to another world.

How the player controls the train

Destination Wheel: Turn the large wheel towards the symbol of an unlocked world.
Speed Lever: Move slowly for sightseeing or move faster for a shorter journey.
Brake Handle: Slow down near views, memory stars, animals, artwork, or stations.
Light Bell: Ring the bell to wake glowing flowers, musical stars, and friendly creatures beside
the track.
Milo Drive: The player can ask Milo to drive automatically at any time.

The view after each world

The railway scenery changes after every completed world. When the player rides past Mind
Spark Kingdom, they can see the clear thought paths and the forest growing again. Near Heart
Melody Kingdom, they can hear their personal song and see musical waterfalls. Near Creator’s
Light Kingdom, they can see their artwork living in the landscape.

This makes the train ride part of the reward. The player can relax, enjoy the view, remember
what they achieved, and choose where the journey goes next.

The Crown of Life

At the end of the journey, the player brings all three treasures to the Maker inside the ARTÉA
Castle. The treasures join together and become the Light of Identity. The Maker shapes this
light into the Crown of Life.

The Crown of Life represents the player’s unique identity, strengths, creativity, purpose, and
story. It does not give the player value. It celebrates the value and light that were already inside
them.

A new star appears in the castle universe with the player’s chosen name or symbol.

3. World One: The Mind Spark Kingdom

  THEME I can understand my thoughts.

Treasure: The Star of Wisdom
Guardian: Everlasting Father, who stands for truth and wisdom

The story

The player enters a dark forest. The trees look like giant computer towers, but leaves and
glowing vines grow around them. Small lights called Thought Sparks float between the trees.

Some Thought Sparks are worries. Some are fears. Some are dreams and hopes. All of them
are mixed together, so the player cannot see a clear path through the forest.

    Milo
    “Every thought is trying to tell us something. We can slow down, look closely, and
    choose where it should go.”

How the game works

Step 1: Find the thought dots: Thoughts appear as glowing dots joined by lines. The player
moves the dots until the lines are clear and easy to follow.
Step 2: Choose the thought: The player chooses one thought, such as “I am not good
enough” or “Something bad will happen.”
Step 3: Move through the gates: The player guides the thought through simple gates. One
gate asks if the thought is a fear. Another asks if there is proof. Another asks what else might be
true.
Step 4: Find a new path: The player chooses a kinder and more helpful thought. For example,
“I am not good enough” can become “I am learning and growing.”
Step 5: Light the forest: The new thought becomes a bright path. Trees wake up, flowers
open, and the player can continue.




                                                                 Simple Story and Game Design | Page 6
ARTÉA | THE JOURNEY TO DISCOVER YOUR LIGHT



The final challenge

At the centre of the forest is a large storm made from many worried thoughts. The player does
not fight the storm. The player slows it down, looks at one thought at a time, and finds a hopeful
path through it.

When the storm becomes gentle rain, the Star of Wisdom grows from the roots of the oldest
tree.

    Everlasting Father
    “You found the courage to understand your thoughts and choose hope.”

What the player learns

  • A thought is not always a fact.

  • Difficult thoughts can be understood.

  • The player can choose a helpful next step.

  • Asking Milo for help is a sign of courage.


4. World Two: The Heart Melody Kingdom

  THEME My feelings have a voice.

Treasure: The Harmony Jewel
Guardian: Archangel Raphael, who stands for healing

The story

The player enters a green valley filled with waterfalls, flowers, and giant musical instruments.
The instruments are connected to glowing machines. The valley should be full of music, but
everything is quiet because the feelings are stuck.

Joy, sadness, frustration, fear, peace, and hope each have their own sound. The player and
Milo must help every feeling find its voice.

    Raphael
    “A healthy heart does not sing only happy songs. Every feeling needs a safe place in the
    music.”

How the game works

The player controls Nature and Magic: The player touches flowers, water, drums, bells, and
glowing strings to express a feeling.
Milo controls Technology: Milo uses machines to turn the feeling into moving platforms,
bridges, lights, and safe paths.
They must work together: The player cannot finish the puzzle alone. Milo cannot finish it
alone. Feeling and support must work together.

Example game moments

  • The player hits large drums to express frustration. Milo turns each drum beat into a solid
     platform.

  • The player plays soft notes for sadness. Milo turns the notes into raindrops that lift a boat.

  • The player rings warning bells for fear. Milo shows which paths are safe and which paths
     need more care.

  • The player grows quiet flowers for peace. Milo slows the machines so the whole valley can
     rest.

  • The player creates a hopeful tune. Milo carries the tune into the sky and wakes the
     morning light.

Building the player’s song

Every puzzle adds one new sound to the music. At the end, the sounds join together. The final
song is different for every player because it is made from their choices.

The Harmony Jewel appears inside the final note. All the feelings can still be seen as colours
inside the jewel.

   Raphael
   “Your feelings matter. Your heart has a beautiful voice.”

What the player learns

  • Feelings are not good or bad.

  • Feelings can be expressed in safe ways.

  • Different feelings can exist together.

5. World Three: The Creator’s Light Kingdom

  THEME I was created to create.

Treasure: The Creator’s Light
Guardian: Uriel, who stands for imagination

The story

The player enters an empty world filled with floating shapes and blank frames. Plants are
waiting inside glass seeds. Rivers are waiting inside silver pipes. The world has everything it
needs, but it needs the player’s imagination to wake up.

Uriel gives the player a glowing frame. Anything drawn inside the frame can become part of the
world.

   Uriel
   “You do not need a perfect idea. Make one small mark, and the world will answer.”

How the game works

Step 1: Milo gives a simple art question: Milo might ask, “Draw a door that leads to your
dreams” or “Draw a place where your feelings can rest.”
Step 2: The player creates art: The player can draw, paint, use shapes, add colours, or
choose simple picture stamps.
Step 3: The art becomes real: The player holds the picture in front of the world. The flat picture
changes into a real object that can be used inside the game.
Step 4: The player explores the new creation: A drawn bridge can be crossed. A drawn door
can be opened. A drawn tree can be climbed. A drawn light can brighten a dark room.
Step 5: Milo asks a gentle question: Milo asks what the player likes, what the picture means,
or what strength the player used. The player can answer, keep it private, or skip.

The final challenge

At the end is a huge empty space called the Perfect Blank. It says that nothing is ready and
nothing is good enough. The player discovers that the answer is not perfect art. The answer is
the courage to begin.

The player places one unfinished picture into the empty space. Milo adds one small light. The
whole world grows around their shared creation.

The Creator’s Light appears as a glowing flame that carries colours from the player’s artwork.

   Uriel
   “Your creativity is a gift that only you can share with the world.”

What the player learns

  • There is no wrong way to be creative.

  • Art does not need to be perfect.

  • Trying again is part of creating.

  • The player’s ideas and story matter.


6. The ARTÉA Castle Finale

  THEME You are uniquely created with purpose.

Returning to the castle

The player returns to ARTÉA Town with the Star of Wisdom, the Harmony Jewel, and the
Creator’s Light. The town now shows everything the player discovered. Bright thought paths
shine above the trees. The player’s song can be heard near the fountain. Their artwork appears
inside the Gallery.

The doors of the ARTÉA Castle open. The Rainbow Galaxy Train enters Celebration Mode. It
carries the player around the complete Crown Line so they can see all three worlds shining
together.

The Light of Identity

Inside the castle, the three treasures float together. The Star brings wisdom. The Jewel brings
feeling and connection. The Flame brings creativity. Together, they become the Light of Identity.

The game remembers the paths the player chose, the way they worked with Milo, the music
they made, and the art they created. It uses these choices to suggest one of 16 game identities
based on the 16 MBTI types.

  IMPORTANT The identity is a guide, not a box. No type is better than another. The player
  can choose a different identity if another one feels more like them.

The Crown of Life

The Maker, who is also called the Prince of Peace, welcomes the player. The Maker reminds
the player that they were already known, loved, and valuable before they found any treasure.

The Maker shapes the three treasures and the player’s identity light into the Crown of Life. The
crown celebrates the player’s thoughts, feelings, creativity, strengths, and growing purpose.

   The Maker
   “These treasures did not make you valuable. They helped you see the light that was
   already inside you.”

The celebration

  • A star appears with the player’s chosen name or symbol.

  • The Rainbow Galaxy Train travels past the thoughts the player changed.

  • The player hears the song they created in Heart Melody Kingdom.

  • The player sees their artwork growing across the universe.

  • Milo celebrates every brave moment and every new discovery.

   Milo
   “Your story matters. You are uniquely created. Your light has a place in this universe.”

8. Milo, the Friendly Journey Guide

Milo travels with the player from the beginning to the end. He helps the player feel safe,
understood, and encouraged. He does not judge the player or tell them what they must feel.




                                                                 Simple Story and Game Design | Page 12
ARTÉA | THE JOURNEY TO DISCOVER YOUR LIGHT



How Milo answers

First, Milo listens: He says, “I hear you” or “Thank you for telling me.”
Next, Milo reflects: He gently names a possible feeling and asks if it fits.
Then, Milo encourages: He reminds the player that one difficult moment does not define who
they are.
After that, Milo offers a choice: The player can talk, visit a world, try a calming activity, pause,
or ask a trusted person for help.
Finally, Milo celebrates growth: He names a strength that the player showed during the
game.

Simple example

    Player
    “Why am I always stressed?”

    Milo
    “It sounds like you have been carrying a lot. Feeling stressed does not mean you are
    weak. Your mind and heart may have been working very hard. Would you like to visit
    Mind Spark Kingdom, take a quiet pause, or think of someone you trust?”

Important care rules

  • Milo is an AI helper, not a real human counsellor.

  • Milo does not give a medical diagnosis.

  • Milo does not ask the player to keep secrets from trusted adults.

  • If a player may be unsafe, Milo helps them contact a trusted adult or the correct support
      service.

  • The player can skip a question and keep artwork private.


9. The Complete Game Journey

Beginning: The player arrives in ARTÉA Town. Milo wakes up and invites the player to
discover their light.
Train journey: The Rainbow Galaxy Train carries the player between the Gallery and each
kingdom. New stations and shortcut routes open as the player completes each world.



                                                                 Simple Story and Game Design | Page 13
ARTÉA | THE JOURNEY TO DISCOVER YOUR LIGHT



World One: The player explores Mind Spark Kingdom, clears the thought paths, changes an
unhelpful thought, and receives the Star of Wisdom.
World Two: The player explores Heart Melody Kingdom, expresses emotions with Milo, builds
a personal song, and receives the Harmony Jewel.
World Three: The player explores Creator’s Light Kingdom, creates art that becomes real, and
receives the Creator’s Light.
Return: The player returns to the ARTÉA Castle. The three treasures become the Light of
Identity.
Identity: The player chooses one of 16 friendly game identities and creates a simple purpose
message.
Celebration: The Maker creates the Crown of Life. A star appears with the player’s name. The
Rainbow Galaxy Train follows the complete railway and celebrates the whole journey.

The main game rule

   DISCOVERY, NOT FIXING The player never earns their value. The player already has
   value. Every activity helps them discover something that was already growing inside them.

What the game should help the player feel

   • I am known.

   • I am loved.

   • My thoughts can be understood.

   • My feelings have a safe voice.

   • My creativity matters.

   • I have strengths.

   • I have a place in this world.

   • My purpose can keep growing.

   FINAL SCREEN MESSAGE Your story matters. You are uniquely created. Your light has
   a place in this universe.
