# MineSpark — Celestial Terrace, direction 03

Status: concept refinement. The user likes direction 02's rich architecture and
steam-engineering mix; this version proposes a more heavenly treatment of that
same design. It is not rebuilt Blender geometry or an integrated world map.

## The feeling

A warm city of invention above the clouds: luminous, peaceful and finely crafted.
Keep the layered stone arches, geometric metalwork and purposeful machinery.
The heavenly quality comes from light, materials and planting, not from replacing
the industrial identity with a different fantasy theme.

## What stays

- The concept's terrace footprint, stairs, bridge landing and basin positions.
- Deep structural arches, layered cornices and tapered piers.
- Reusable stone courses, brass panels, ribs, bearings, pipes and fasteners.
- Recognizable piston, shaft, flywheel and valve relationships.
- Existing game map, route, quest logic and all Blender assets remain untouched.

Preserving the concept composition does not establish that it matches the live
map's dimensions. Fitting assets to the unchanged game layout is a later step.

## What becomes heavenly

| Element | Proposed treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Civic stone | Warm ivory, alabaster-like highlights, softly shaded carved recesses |
| Brass | Satin champagne gold with subtle edge highlights, fewer heavy dark borders |
| Structural iron | Pearl/ivory enamel outer ribs, bronze retained inside machinery |
| Steam pipes | Lighter rose copper, deliberate connections, thin clean mist |
| Lamps and instruments | Opalescent glass, restrained honey-gold light |
| Floor | Cream stone, warm radial gold inlay, clear flat walking surface |
| Basin | Pale aquamarine reflections, pearl-and-gold coping |
| Planting | Fresh sage leaves and delicate ivory blossoms within the same beds |
| Island underside | Pale stratified limestone with shaded blue-gray pockets and fine mineral seams |
| Atmosphere | Peach-gold sunrise, pale blue sky and clouds below the island |

Suggested colors: pearl `#F7F0DF`, warm ivory `#E9DFC8`, champagne gold `#CEB477`,
rose copper `#BE8C73`, sage `#A5B69B`, pale aquamarine `#B5DEDB`, shadow blue-gray
`#7D8D9B`. These are proposed art-direction swatches, not calibrated engine values.

## Guardrails for eventual Blender work

- Preserve the detail and silhouette the user liked. A brighter material pass
  cannot replace modeling the arch profiles, layered edges and machinery.
- Keep enough shadow contrast for stone joints and mechanical parts to read.
- Do not make every material emissive or apply bloom across the entire scene.
- Clouds belong below/background; they must not hide missing geometry or routes.
- Retain a coherent stone/metal/pipe assembly system; do not substitute crystals
  or disconnected decorative gears for steam mechanisms.
- Triangles remain editable mesh topology, not a wireframe decoration on floors.
- No added religious figures, new towers, or changes to the map layout.
