# ARTÉA: The Crown of Life — Complete Story Bible

Status: canonical narrative for the connected campaign. Stable implementation
IDs, exact prerequisites, completion evidence, paired dialogue, and replay
rules live in `data/crown_of_life_story.json`; this document is its readable
dramatic and production guide.

## The promise

ARTÉA Castle is not broken, and neither is the player. Its three celestial
roads have become quiet because they respond only to discoveries that are
freely noticed, expressed, and created. The player becomes the Lightbearer: not
someone sent to earn worth, but someone invited to discover wisdom, harmony,
and creativity already alive within them.

Each kingdom teaches a different way of seeing. Each treasure changes the
Castle and makes the next road possible. Only together can the Star of Wisdom,
Harmony Jewel, and Creator's Light become the Light of Identity. The Maker in
the faith presentation—or the ARTÉA Celebrant in the universal presentation—
then shapes that light into the Crown of Life.

The causal journey is:

```text
Notice and choose a thought
  → the Star of Wisdom reveals how to listen
Express and make room for feelings
  → the Harmony Jewel tunes the Galaxy Train
Create and frame a unique work
  → Creator's Light awakens the Gallery
Bring all three discoveries home
  → Light of Identity → Crown of Life → the player's star
```

## Prologue — The Castle of Listening Light

The player arrives in a dark-blue gallery that opens into a universe of brass
observatories, constellation circuits, stained light, music, memory, and art.
Milo shows three quiet roads: the Thought Constellation, Heart Resonance Rail,
and Creator's Prism Path. None responds to force. They awaken only through the
player's own safe, deliberate actions.

Milo offers Continue, Not yet, and Explore controls. “Not yet” is a complete,
respected choice; it never changes the player's value or deletes progress.

- Faith: “The Maker knows your light already. Nothing in you is a missing
  piece. Will you discover the gifts placed within you?”
- Universal: “Your light is already part of you. Nothing in you is a missing
  piece. Will you discover your gifts?”

Accepting the invitation lights the Mind Spark route. It does not grant a
treasure or fabricate personal data.

## Substory I — Mind Spark Kingdom

Theme: “I can notice my thoughts and choose a helpful next thought.”

The Thought Sky is full of authored clouds. Some sound worried, rushed, or
small; others carry dreams and hopes. They are story objects, not diagnoses or
questions about the player. Everlasting Father in faith mode, or the Keeper of
Wisdom in universal mode, teaches that a thought may be noticed without being
obeyed or becoming an identity.

### Quest 1: Whispers in the Thought Sky (`mind.quest.listen`)

Purpose: practise observing a thought without treating it as a fact.

1. `mind.listen.notice` — **Notice a Thought Cloud.** Choose an authored cloud
   (or the neutral “A thought is here” card) and hold it in the lens. This
   establishes agency: the player chooses what enters the encounter.
2. `mind.listen.name` — **Name What the Cloud Does.** Choose a curated effect
   such as smaller, louder, rushed, hopeful, or unclear. The game records only
   the authored choice, never an inference about the child.
3. `mind.listen.release` — **Give the Cloud Some Space.** Move the cloud beside
   the player instead of destroying it. This visually proves that noticing can
   create distance without calling a thought bad.

The moved cloud reveals two observatory windows. That physical change causes
the next quest to begin.

### Quest 2: The Two Windows of Truth (`mind.quest.reframe`)

Purpose: choose a believable, compassionate next thought rather than forced
positivity.

1. `mind.reframe.compare` — **Open Both Windows.** View the difficult authored
   thought beside two truthful, hopeful possibilities. Both are safe; there is
   no punitive wrong answer.
2. `mind.reframe.choose` — **Choose a Growing Thought.** Select a line such as
   “I am learning and growing” or “I can ask for help.” The action provides
   honest JourneyState evidence but does not yet award the kingdom treasure.
3. `mind.reframe.bridge` — **Cross the Hope Bridge.** The chosen words become
   constellation platforms. Crossing them turns reflection into embodied
   courage and opens the Wisdom Observatory.

### Quest 3: The Wisdom Constellation (`mind.quest.guardian`)

Purpose: consolidate Notice, Name, and Choose as a repeatable strength.

1. `mind.guardian.trace` — **Trace Your Three Lights.** Connect the three
   symbols in any visual pattern; variation changes beauty, not success.
2. `mind.guardian.receive` — **Receive the Star of Wisdom.** “Receive now” and
   “Place it nearby” are equivalent choices. The Star is awarded only after at
   least two selected Thought Echoes have actually been resolved.

The Star illuminates the Castle observatory. Its light does not solve the next
world; it reveals that wisdom includes listening, causing the Heart rail to
appear.

### Castle return I — Wisdom Becomes a Road

Milo places the Star in the map. A pulse travels from the observatory to the
silent Heart Resonance Rail. The player may rest, revisit Mind, or continue.
Returning is the required causal interlude; merely entering Mind never unlocks
Heart.

## Substory II — Heart Melody Kingdom

Theme: “My feelings have a voice.”

The Duet Garden has not lost emotion; its instruments have gone quiet because
no single melody can speak for every feeling. Archangel Raphael in faith mode,
or the Keeper of Healing in universal mode, invites the player to listen,
answer, and make room for more than one voice.

### Quest 1: The Instruments That Went Quiet (`heart.quest.voices`)

Purpose: associate feelings with chosen musical language while keeping the
player—not the game—as the source of meaning.

1. `heart.voices.listen` — **Listen Without Guessing.** Preview at least three
   motifs among Joy, Sadness, Frustration, Fear, Peace, and Hope. Colour,
   symbol, vibration, and caption make every motif readable without sound.
2. `heart.voices.choose` — **Give One Feeling a Voice.** Choose a feeling for a
   fictional garden scene and select its instrument. The game never claims it
   is the player's real feeling.
3. `heart.voices.echo` — **Answer the Garden's Echo.** Repeat or simplify a
   short rhythm with visible timing help and an assisted-completion option.

The answered echo grows the first musical path, causing the duet bridge to
open.

### Quest 2: The Bridge That Needs Two Songs (`heart.quest.duet`)

Purpose: practise empathy and cooperation by preserving two distinct voices.

1. `heart.duet.first_voice` — **Carry the First Melody.** Guide one motif
   through cooperative platforms while Milo or a second local player holds
   the response.
2. `heart.duet.swap` — **Hear the Other Side.** Swap Listener and Composer so
   the other motif reveals paths that were invisible before.
3. `heart.duet.resolve` — **Make Room for Both.** Adjust volume and tempo until
   both remain audible in a player-chosen balance. There is no perfect mix and
   no feeling is required to disappear.

The balanced duet powers the garden conductor and opens the final constellation.

### Quest 3: The Chord of Belonging (`heart.quest.harmony`)

Purpose: celebrate expression and give the Rainbow Galaxy Train its harmonic
engine.

1. `heart.harmony.compose` — **Compose the Heart Constellation.** Place at
   least two unlocked motifs in a short loop. Free timing and assisted timing
   reach the same result.
2. `heart.harmony.receive` — **Receive the Harmony Jewel.** “Carry it” and “Let
   the train carry it” both award the Jewel from real authored interaction.

### Castle return II — Harmony Tunes the Stars

The Jewel enters the rail engine. The Galaxy Train answers with a two-note
call: Wisdom has learned to listen, and Harmony can now carry a creative spark.
The Creator's Prism Path unfolds from the combined star and chord.

## Substory III — Creator's Light Kingdom

Theme: “I was created to create.”

Uriel, Keeper of Creativity, waits in a science-magic atelier where drawings
become stained-light maps. The world never scores, diagnoses, or interprets
art. Meaning belongs to the player; every authored creation route succeeds.

### Quest 1: The Door No Map Could Draw (`creator.quest.spark`)

Purpose: begin from choice and protect ownership of creative meaning.

1. `creator.spark.prompt` — **Choose Your Creative Door.** Select a curated
   prompt from Identity, Courage, Emotions, Creativity, or Purpose—or choose a
   mystery prompt. Prompts remain skippable and offline.
2. `creator.spark.create` — **Make a Light Only You Would Make.** Add marks,
   shapes, colours, or accessible stamps with the local ARTÉA tools. Any mark
   is enough; quality is never measured.
3. `creator.spark.save` — **Name the Moment.** Save locally with a curated
   title, an optional custom title, or “Untitled Light.” Saving records a
   creation but deliberately does not award Creator's Light.

The saved light becomes a frame tile and causes the Living Frame chamber to
open.

### Quest 2: Step Through the Living Frame (`creator.quest.view`)

Purpose: turn creative choices into safe spatial discovery without copying
another game's photo-to-3D mechanic.

1. `creator.view.place` — **Place the Frame.** Align the local artwork with one
   of several authored scene anchors. Preview and unlimited Undo prevent dead
   ends.
2. `creator.view.transform` — **Walk Into Your Perspective.** Confirming opens
   a prepared ARTÉA micro-scene derived from authored mappings, not AI image
   analysis.
3. `creator.view.discover` — **Find the Unplanned Wonder.** Touch one authored
   detail and choose what kind of wonder it represents. The choice changes
   ornament and colour, never access or reward.

The discovered wonder returns as a motif for the Gallery Frame Ritual.

### Quest 3: The Gallery Frame Ritual (`creator.quest.reflect`)

Purpose: support self-reflection while refusing automated conclusions.

1. `creator.reflect.choose` — **Choose What You Want to Notice.** Select a
   curated card such as proud part, strength used, story told, or keep it a
   mystery. This task is optional and cannot block progress.
2. `creator.reflect.frame` — **Place Your Work Among the Stars.** Select Star
   Bridge, Dream Door, or Constellation Mural; preview, place, undo, and confirm.
3. `creator.reflect.receive` — **Receive the Creator's Light.** “Hold the
   Light” and “Let it shine from the artwork” are equivalent. Only confirmed
   frame placement awards the treasure.

### Castle return III — The Gallery Awakens

The framed creation joins the Castle without being judged. Creator's Light
travels through the Star of Wisdom and Harmony Jewel. Their three motifs form
one beam: the Light of Identity. The finale threshold opens only when all three
canonical treasures are present.

## Finale — The Crown of Life

1. **Convergence.** The three treasures orbit the observatory and become the
   Light of Identity. No generated personality summary appears.
2. **The remembered gallery.** The Castle shows real completion moments and,
   only when locally enabled, the player's artwork and chosen colours.
3. **Rainbow Galaxy Train.** A player-controlled celebration passes the three
   transformed kingdoms. Skip, captions, mute-readable effects, and a static
   reduced-motion alternative preserve the complete outcome.
4. **The Crown hall.** The Maker/Prince of Peace in faith mode, or the ARTÉA
   Celebrant in universal mode, shapes—not awards—the Crown from treasures the
   player has already discovered.
5. **The name star.** With explicit local opt-in, a display name appears on a
   new star. Otherwise it is “Your Star.” The name is never required or sent
   to a network service.

Final message:

> You are known. You are valued. You have unique gifts. Your creativity has a
> place in this universe. Your story matters.

The faith presentation roots this dignity in being uniquely created and known
by God. The universal presentation expresses the same dignity, purpose, and
belonging without faith-specific names. Puzzles, timing, access, treasures,
Crown, and replay outcomes are identical.

## Epilogue and replay

The Castle remains awake. The player may revisit any completed kingdom, remix
music, make new art, or ride the train. Replay never duplicates treasures or
deletes artwork. A full reset requires separate adult-visible confirmation and
does not silently erase local creations. The optional Lens story is a replay
experience only; it records Lens chapters and perception but cannot award the
three canonical treasures or bypass the campaign.

## Production acceptance

- Exactly three ordered substories, nine quests, and twenty-five authored
  tasks match the catalog IDs.
- Every task has a gameplay action, developmental purpose, causal story
  reason, prerequisite, completion evidence, and retry/skip/replay policy in
  the JSON source of truth.
- Entering a scene never completes a task or awards a treasure.
- Star of Wisdom requires the full curated Mind encounter; Harmony Jewel
  follows an explicit authored emotion/music action; Creator's Light requires
  confirmed frame placement.
- Faith and universal modes change authored presentation only.
- No required path accepts free-text disclosure, infers emotion, evaluates
  art, calls a model, uses a network request, or requires a name/share action.
- Keyboard/controller focus, captions, mute readability, colour-independent
  symbols, timing assist, undo, pause, skip, and reduced motion are covered.
- The quest journal may display canonical treasure state but cannot invent
  task completion or write personal data.
