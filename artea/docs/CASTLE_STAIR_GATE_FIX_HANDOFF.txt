Root causes confirmed and fixed, all in scripts/hub_castle_stairs.gd:

1. Crown slab blocking the stair (defect 1). CrownDeck was a full solid
   CylinderMesh (radius 8.4 = the spiral's own outer tread edge), so every
   loop of the spiral passes under its footprint, and its underside sat only
   ~0.5 m above the final treads. Replaced with a hand-built ArrayMesh disc
   (_crown_deck_mesh) that has a genuine wedge-shaped stairwell cut out. The
   cutout's centre/half-angle are now DERIVED from the actual climb (matching
   _build_stair's angle math) so the opening covers every tread within
   MIN_HEAD_CLEARANCE (2.4 m) of the deck's underside, not just the single
   final tread — first attempt only widened around the last angle and still
   failed a treads-30m-back regression; fixed by centering the wedge one
   half-swept-angle behind stair_end_angle instead of on top of it. A brass
   StairwellKerb lines both open edges so the gap reads as a deliberate
   opening. The parapet gap at the stair mouth now matches the same widened
   span so the floor opening and the wall opening line up.

2. Village houses beside/inside the stair envelope (defect 2). Confirmed with
   scripts/diag_castle_stair_obstructions.gd: building_home_A_red under
   District_HeartMelody/hm_ext_home_3 sat 13.4 m from the tower base, outside
   the old clear_radius (~9.9 m circle around the tower only). _clear_obstructions
   now checks a real corridor: the tower's circular footprint PLUS a capsule
   along the bridge/causeway span (crown gateway to terrace target, half-width
   BRIDGE_WIDTH/2 + 1.5 m), so props near the bridge are caught too, not just
   the spiral. Added PROTECTED_ANCESTOR_NAMES (player, castle, entrance, crown,
   citadel, atlas, skyway, portal, hub lens, global, spawners, magitech,
   landmark glow) checked via _is_protected() before anything is queued for
   removal, so gameplay-critical systems can never be swept up even if the
   corridor geometry changes later.

3. Loose/crossing rail pieces (defect 3). No separate defect found beyond the
   crown/bridge geometry already covered by 1 and 4 — the continuous helix
   guards and bridge rails already terminate cleanly at open gate mouths.

4. Weak crown-to-gate connection (defect 4). The crown side already had a lit
   pier-and-lintel gateway; the terrace side only had a bare landing pad with
   one beacon light, so the bridge read as finished on one end and stubbed on
   the other. Added _build_terrace_portal: matching piers, lanterns and a
   brass lintel at the terrace end, set back by the bridge's own gate
   clearance so it sits in the already-open rail mouth instead of narrowing it.

5. LUMEN ASCENT sign overlap (defect 5). Was a large (font 28) billboard
   centred directly on the stair's entry radius/angle, facing the player
   head-on on every approach. Moved to a quarter-turn off-axis, pushed out
