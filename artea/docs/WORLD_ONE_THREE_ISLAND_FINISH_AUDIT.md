## What still reads as a prototype

### Island 1

The arrival conservatory and underside machinery have the strongest authored silhouette of the three islands. The opposite tree court now has a small gathering crescent, but its broad floor still needs a more readable relationship between the tree, compass and quest controls; avoid adding more freestanding gold trim. The sixteen learning instruments are functionally distinct, but several small controls and captions are difficult to read from the normal gameplay camera. The global visible-mesh collision pass needs a selective, explicit architecture inventory rather than a broad rule that has to exclude one floor detail at a time. The quality-safety test covers all sixteen control and helper solids and gate sweeps; the new all-quest traversal separately proves the continuous spawn-to-return route.

### Island 2

The domed observatories, bridge and structural undercroft look coherent at a distance. The synthesis table anchors the evidence court, the open Echo Seed reliquary no longer blocks its player camera, and the five live instruments have legible approach markers. The live Understanding Shrine now gives the Conservatory a visible completion state without retaining the retired Guardian Court. Those markers still share one frame design; each task's active and solved state needs a normal-camera review. The Root Stalker is a first authored-creature replacement, not a completed enemy-art pass: its side and hero silhouettes read clearly, while its top silhouette is weak and it needs an in-context animation and combat readability review. The five Blender GLBs now register their legacy atlas UIDs before loading, and both the native fixture and deployed Web build resolve the checked-in textures without path-fallback warnings. Preserve the current save IDs and demonstrated story behavior.

### Island 3

The fused foundation and route work, and the quieter parapet and pale perimeter saplings leave the landmarks easier to see. The three storm quest buildings now have a clear size and shape hierarchy, with open console forecourts and deliberate negative space around the Warden fight. The muted garden beds recede behind their entrances in the latest normal-camera capture. The Oldest Tree shares Island 1's calm palette and exposes its boughs; the new living-grove promenade and leafy reflection pavilion now create a continuous visual transition into it. The role-aware audit classifies all 325 hidden meshes as dormant quest, enemy or completion states and finds zero retained retired meshes. Preserve the active Oldest Tree, its collision, the rain/recovery sequence, and saved quest state.

The latest player-height comparison (`artifacts/mind_spark_island_three_low_poly/06_player_arrival.png` against Island 1's garden view and Island 2's overview) shows the three new midground silhouettes framing the Warden core while the foreground and console approaches remain open. The court is still physically broad, but it now uses the same authored response as the earlier islands: large architecture, mature trees, and perimeter pavilions establish scale rather than extra shrubs, markers, or gold/cyan lines.

## Finish criteria before calling any island final

1. The native fixture now completes all fifteen individual mini-games through their player input handlers and checks touch controls in every mode. Run fresh, partial and completed-save browser playthroughs to verify quest order, Warden shield/core, gentle rain, reflection choice, Oldest Tree completion, and real touch/controller ergonomics in the deployed build.
2. Keep the matched player-height capture set current through `scripts/capture_world_one_matched_player_views.gd`. The first cross-island review and resulting Island 3 palette revision are complete; repeat it whenever landmark placement, sign scale, occlusion, foliage, or quest interaction positions change.
3. Keep the role-aware retired-geometry gate at zero. The imported World One texture UIDs and Cloud Pop runtime references are fixed. All 29 duplicate UID groups and the stale start-screen music UID are resolved; `scripts/check_unique_resource_uids.py` now blocks a Web export if duplicate identities return. Keep Web exports free of runtime resource and script errors.
4. Benchmark the deployed WebGL2 build on the target hardware: startup memory, load time, frame rate in each district and during mini-games, and controller/touch usability. A native headless pass does not establish Web performance or visual quality.

This audit intentionally separates demonstrated fixes from remaining art and playtest work. A numerical 10/10 claim needs those criteria and a real player review; procedural mesh count and passing hierarchy assertions alone cannot establish it.
