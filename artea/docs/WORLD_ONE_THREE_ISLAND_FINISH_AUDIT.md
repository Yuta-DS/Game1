## What still reads as a prototype

### Island 1

The arrival conservatory and underside machinery have the strongest authored silhouette of the three islands. The opposite tree court now has a small gathering crescent, but its broad floor still needs a more readable relationship between the tree, compass and quest controls; avoid adding more freestanding gold trim. The sixteen learning instruments are functionally distinct, but several small controls and captions are difficult to read from the normal gameplay camera. The global visible-mesh collision pass needs a selective, explicit architecture inventory rather than a broad rule that has to exclude one floor detail at a time. The quality-safety test covers all sixteen control and helper solids and gate sweeps; the new all-quest traversal separately proves the continuous spawn-to-return route.

### Island 2

The domed observatories, bridge and structural undercroft look coherent at a distance. The synthesis table anchors the evidence court, the open Echo Seed reliquary no longer blocks its player camera, and the five live instruments have distinct approach markers whose crowns communicate evidence, memory, logic, perspective and truth. The inset state jewel and small wing/leaf accents now expose inactive, active and completed states; a real scanner interaction regression verifies all three. The live Understanding Shrine gives the Conservatory a visible completion state without retaining the retired Guardian Court. The completed chapter no longer allocates the retired constellation and Hope Path combat actors, leaving no invisible hitboxes or empty actor shells in the research route. The five Blender GLBs now register their legacy atlas UIDs before loading, and both the native fixture and deployed Web build resolve the checked-in textures without path-fallback warnings. Preserve the current save IDs and demonstrated story behavior.

### Island 3

The fused foundation and route work, and the quieter parapet and pale perimeter saplings leave the landmarks easier to see. The three storm quest buildings now have a clear size and shape hierarchy, with open console forecourts and deliberate negative space around the Warden fight. The Warden itself now uses a compact ivory, charcoal and cyan storm-engine silhouette instead of the oversized purple ring cluster, so it no longer blocks the court hierarchy in the matched arrival camera. The muted garden beds recede behind their entrances, while two distant sky gardens give the formerly empty horizon a restrained second layer without adding collision or another playable island. The Oldest Tree shares Island 1's calm palette and exposes its boughs; the new living-grove promenade and leafy reflection pavilion now create a continuous visual transition into it. The role-aware audit classifies all hidden meshes as dormant quest, enemy or completion states and rejects retained retired meshes. Preserve the active Oldest Tree, its collision, the rain/recovery sequence, and saved quest state.

The latest player-height comparison (`artifacts/mind_spark_island_three_low_poly/06_player_arrival.png` against Island 1's garden view and Island 2's overview) shows the three new midground silhouettes framing the Warden core while the foreground and console approaches remain open. The court is still physically broad, but it now uses the same authored response as the earlier islands: large architecture, mature trees, and perimeter pavilions establish scale rather than extra shrubs, markers, or gold/cyan lines.
