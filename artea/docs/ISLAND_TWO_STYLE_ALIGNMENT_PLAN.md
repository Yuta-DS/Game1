# Island Two — Island One Style Alignment Plan

## Design decision

Island Two should inherit Island One's **design grammar**, not copy its layout.
Keep Island Two's existing fused footprint, quest order, save IDs, Memory Gate,
and foundation machinery. Redesign the upper deck as the **Observatory of
Understanding**: a warm heavenly research campus where evidence is read,
memories are connected, perspective is changed, and hope becomes structurally
solid.

The target remains stylized low-poly: generous silhouettes, economical geometry,
quiet large surfaces, and concentrated detail at joints, controls, lenses, and
rewards. It is not a request for thousands of unrelated decorative objects.

## What Island One gets right

| Island One strength | Why it works | Island Two translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One dominant landmark per district | The glass laboratory and Wisdom Tree make each half identifiable at a glance. | Give Evidence Court a celestial archive/orrery canopy and Hope Ridge a lens pavilion with a smaller guardian terminus. |
| Quest machines sit inside a department | Instruments look like one laboratory rather than random props. | Group Scanner, Memory Board, Bell, and Logic Gate into one Evidence Observatory; group Perspective Lens and Truth Bridge into one Hope Gallery. |
| Shared material roles | Ivory is structure, brass is mechanism, teal is recess/enamel, aqua is active energy. | Use the same roles and roughness response; do not colour whole machines cyan, purple, or gold. |
| Repeated parts with different silhouettes | Plinths, lanterns, rails, cabinets, and fittings repeat, but each final machine reads differently. | Reuse the kit while giving all six stations a unique mechanism and profile. |
| A clear working aisle | Controls face the player and have visible operator space. | Retain the canonical Island Two approach anchors and build the architecture around them. |
| Grouped landscaping | Planting frames activity without occupying every empty space. | Replace evenly scattered purple trees with three deliberate planting families and clear courts. |
| Physical service relationships | Garden instruments feed a visible receiving manifold. | Build one Evidence Aether Bus and one Hope Materialisation Conduit with supported endpoints. |
| Restrained state lighting | Emission indicates availability, operation, and completion. | Use dormant teal, active aqua, and completion gold/hope green only on feedback components. |

## Current Island Two gaps

The existing foundation is strong: its below-deck engine bays, arched structural
shell, towers, and circular deck already belong to the same world as Island One.
The upper deck is the weak layer.

- The large Evidence deck has no dominant architectural centre.
- Quest stations are readable individually but look scattered at overview scale.
- Repeated purple trees and small spheres do not form meaningful planting groups.
- The Hope side is dense with unrelated ring shapes and rectangular platforms.
- Strong cyan and purple accents compete with quest feedback.
- Machinery below the deck is detailed, while the playable deck uses simpler
  prototype silhouettes; this creates a visible quality discontinuity.
- There is no single visible service system explaining how Evidence powers the
  Memory Gate and how Hope powers the final materialisation.
- The latest source layout is newer than the current overview render, so a new
  baseline capture is required before judging exact spacing.

## Target spatial composition

```text
ISLAND ONE BRIDGE
       ↓
[A] ECHO-SEED RECEPTION
       ↓
[B] EVIDENCE OBSERVATORY
    Scanner → Memory Loom → Archive Verifier
         ↘ optional Resonance Bell
       ↓
[C] HINGED MEMORY GATE + PROTECTED PROMENADE
       ↓
[D] HOPE OPTICS GALLERY
    Perspective Lens → Truth Materialiser
       ↓
[E] GUARDIAN / CHAPTER COMPLETION COURT
       ↓
NEXT ISLAND CONNECTION
```

### A. Echo-seed reception

- Keep the existing receiver near the arrival route.
- Replace the small stand-alone reading with a supported receiving cabinet:
  stepped ivory base, teal recessed chamber, brass capture forks, and one aqua
  seed capsule.
- Add a short physical conduit into the Evidence Aether Bus.
- Keep a clear arrival cone so the player sees the receiver and Observatory
  landmark together.

### B. Evidence Observatory

- Establish a central **Celestial Archive Orrery** as the district landmark.
  It is an architectural machine, not another clickable quest object.
- Use a partial ribbed aqua-glass canopy, open toward the arrival and Memory Gate,
  so player sightlines and the sky remain visible.
- Organize the four stations as research bays around one calm work court:
  Evidence Scanner west, Memory Loom centre, Archive Verifier east, optional
  Resonance Bell in a quiet southern alcove.
- Replace individual isolated pads with connected ivory floor insets and narrow
  brass measurement rules. The route remains discrete, not a solid coloured line.
- Put the shared Evidence Aether Bus behind or below the stations. Every pipe or
  light channel needs a named source, supported route, and destination.

### C. Memory Gate and promenade

- Keep one progression gate only: the existing two-leaf hinged Memory Gate.
- Add moulded side piers, visible hinge motors, a lintel archive, and a floor
  threshold that matches Island One's gate construction language.
- Keep the leaf swept volumes empty. No planters, labels, lamps, or conduits may
  enter the opening envelope.
- Continue Island One's railing grammar across the promenade: substantial ivory
  posts, champagne-brass rails, and warm lanterns at rhythmically spaced bays.
- Keep the already-connected foundation; do not add a redundant deployable bridge.

### D. Hope Optics Gallery

- Make the Hope half visually lighter and more vertical than Evidence Court.
- Build a **Lens Pavilion** from three asymmetrical ivory ribs holding suspended
  aqua lenses. Avoid another complete circular ring as the main silhouette.
- Turn the Perspective Lens into an optical carousel with three framed view
  planes. Rotation must visibly alter the same scene or statement.
- Turn the Truth Bridge station into a materialisation engine with three courses:
  ivory foundation, teal stabilization field, and restrained gold completion cap.
- Replace the current loose rectangular pads with a stepped sequence of shaped
  terraces. Preserve the existing walkable surface and avoid precision jumps.
- Use small groups of pale-gold canopy trees, low mint shrubs, and lavender
  thought flowers. Lavender remains a minor secondary accent.

### E. Guardian court

- Keep the encounter after the reasoning stations and outside every interaction
  apron.
- Frame it with two luminous pylons and a shallow observatory exedra so it reads
  as a deliberate conclusion, not enemies placed on spare floor.
- Show a direct sightline from the Truth Materialiser to the court's reward socket.
- Keep combat clearance and the route to the next connection physically open.

## Quest-object redesign briefs

| Existing station | Final architectural object | Required visible mechanism | Reusable components |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence Scanner | Evidence Spectrometer | Three fragments enter a framed lens; the lens separates fact from prediction. | Ivory instrument plinth, brass bearing, teal cabinet, aqua lens |
| Memory Board | Memory Loom | Three physical cards travel on a rail and lock into Notice → Name → Choose order. | Card socket, carriage rail, plaque, completion lamp |
| Focus Bell | Resonance Bell | Bell, damper, and graduated echo chamber reveal one calm hint after activation. | Bearing collar, small chamber, lever, lantern lens |
| Logic Gate | Archive Verifier | Two evidence inputs engage one mechanical key and release the gate signal. | Twin input socket, key drum, teal recess, output conduit |
| Perspective Lens | Perspective Carousel | Three offset view planes rotate around one supported optical axis. | Lens frame, pivot, hand grip, sight hood |
| Truth Bridge | Truth Materialiser | Three stable courses assemble in sequence and send energy toward the guardian court. | Course tray, lifting piston, field lens, reward socket |

No two stations may use a decorative ring as their primary silhouette. Shared
parts should communicate a common manufacturer; proportions, mechanisms, and
profiles must communicate different purposes.

## Reusable Island Two kit

Build or extract these components once, then instance them:

1. Stepped ivory station plinth: narrow, standard, and landmark widths.
2. Teal recessed cabinet panel: dormant, active, and complete material states.
3. Champagne-brass bearing and pivot family.
4. Supported conduit kit: straight pipe, elbow, floor socket, wall clamp, and
   intentionally non-solid light channel.
5. Aqua optical lens family: small indicator, operating lens, canopy glass.
6. Observatory rib: straight, shallow arch, and tall pavilion variants.
7. Railing bay: straight, curved, terminal, gate-clearance, and bridge variants.
8. Lantern pier: court, promenade, and guardian heights.
9. Mounted department sign and small machine plaque.
10. Planter edge with pale tree, mint shrub, and lavender-flower variants.
11. Operator apron inset and discrete guidance facet.
12. Interaction/camera anchors plus simple collision-envelope templates.

Fixed pieces sharing materials may be batched. Moving mechanisms, state lenses,
doors, and independently hidden parts remain separate. Reuse source components;
do not duplicate whole final stations.

## Materials and lighting

- Preserve the 70/20/10 balance: about 70% warm ivory structure, 20% teal/glass/
  secondary materials, and 10% brass, copper, lavender, or state accents.
- Reduce pure-white floor clipping. Add subtle value separation between deck,
  mouldings, stations, and pale foliage.
- Use contact shadows and ambient occlusion at plinths, rail posts, canopy feet,
  and machinery joints.
- Evidence Observatory: neutral-warm key light with restrained aqua task lights.
- Memory Gate: warmer threshold light that becomes brighter only when unlocked.
- Hope Gallery: warm upper light, pale aqua fill, and restrained hope-green success.
- Keep emission local to lenses, readings, conduits, and reward states.
- Apply the same camera-local environment method used by Island One instead of
  changing the global environment for every world.

## Physics and player experience

- Preserve all six existing save IDs, prerequisites, valid solutions, revision
  behavior, and reload restoration.
- Build every station around its canonical approach anchor and minimum four-metre
  apron. Verify the real player capsule, not only a point position.
- Station housings, piers, cabinets, and reachable displays receive simplified
  static collision matching their useful silhouette.
- Doors and moving mechanisms keep collision attached to the moving assembly.
- Light, holograms, and floor guidance remain intentionally non-solid.
- Route reachable pipes below the floor or behind guarded service rails.
- Test the full inward/outward Memory Gate sweep with occupied space on both leaves.
- Avoid foliage, signs, or labels inside the first-person viewing cone.
- Reuse Island One's compact contextual side panel so the machine remains visible
  while the player reasons.

## Acceptance criteria

Island Two matches Island One's quality only when:

1. A player can identify Evidence Observatory, Memory Gate, Hope Gallery, and
   Guardian Court from one overview without relying on floating labels.
2. Every station has a unique purpose-readable silhouette and clear operator side.
3. All visible structural and service elements have believable support and endpoints.
4. The palette is shared with Island One while the observatory architecture remains
   visibly distinct from Island One's greenhouse and garden.
5. Quest state, revision, completion, gate motion, collision, and reload behavior pass.
6. The full route is verified from the player camera in native and web builds.

This plan changes no gameplay contract and authorizes no merge, commit, push, or
deployment by itself.
