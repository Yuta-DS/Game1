# Island One — Heavenly Research Campus

## Story and layout

Preserve the three-island map, central trees, ivory/gold/teal low-poly style,
five-quest campaign and saved-state keys. Only the two districts of Island One
are redesigned. No new mandatory puzzles, combat, jumps or bridges.

| Area | Story purpose | Layout and approach |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival / Signal Laboratory | Notice and investigate a thought through light experiments | Optics instruments face the shared work court. Analysis follows the three experiments; regulators and alignment tools face the player's approach. |
| Internal promenade | Carry the earned Signal Prism forward | Existing heavenly gate opens after Quest 1, not after Quest 2. The existing solid walkway remains. |
| Garden / Living Research Laboratory | Examine echoes, distinguish evidence from possibility, choose a helpful response | All six Echo controls and their sockets, readings, rewards and conduits move together into the garden's western laboratory court. Desks face its central working aisle. |
| Garden relay / Island Two exit | Consolidate learning and continue | Checkpoint and spark relay face the garden approach. Existing campaign requirements still lock the external exit. |

The garden laboratory preserves its local six-station arrangement around
(-52,24), translating that centre to (-28,40) in the Living Network district.
This keeps the control-to-orb/conduit relationships intact and leaves the mature
tree at the garden centre untouched. Runtime control paths remain under the
original PuzzleControls parent, so hints and proximity interactions still resolve.

## Environment language

Use a warm civic laboratory, not a dark industrial factory: glass specimen
chambers, brass utility rails, ivory sample archives, mint indicators, botanical
research displays and clear department signs. Reuse existing textured Blender
assets for display cabinets. Display furniture has no quest interaction and is
labelled as reference material. Preserve main walkways and mature trees.
