# Island Two Legacy Recovery Audit

## Finding

Revision 02 preserved the quest mechanics but removed too much of the older environmental composition. It hid the complete Aether Lift Pavilion, curved sunrise gardens, both fourteen-tree groves, eight memory lights, and all Hope Path thought flowers. This created large empty areas and weakened Island Two's hierarchy, scale, and visual continuity.

## Revision 04 decision

The recovery is an integration, not a rollback.

### Restored

- Aether Lift Pavilion ivory dais and eight-column civic silhouette.
- Segmented sunrise canopy, open canopy ribs, lift core, and opal crown.
- Existing twin condenser engine houses and their foundation-powering machinery.
- Twelve curved sunrise garden terraces.
- Hope Path thought flowers.
- All eight archive memory lanterns from the original memory-light ring, resized to support rather than overpower the quest stations.
- All mature trees from both original fourteen-tree groves.
- Six portal-support piers and their brass inlays, repurposed as a three-bay Hall of Hope archive colonnade. These were incorrectly hidden by a broad `TruthPortal_*` wildcard.

### Still removed

- Oversized Aether Lift halo and halo rays.
- Three older truth-portal rings that duplicate the new Truth Foundry.
- Duplicate Proof/Logic Gate.
- Generic internal bridge slabs and solid route lines replaced by the protected Memory Promenade.

### Preserved from the new implementation

- Echo Seed continuation and nine story states.
- Evidence Laboratory, Memory Atelier, Archive Engine, Perspective Laboratory, and Truth Foundry.
- Evidence Aether Bus and Hope Materialisation Conduit.
- Hinged Memory Gate and save-compatible quest IDs.
- Player-facing interaction panels, wrong-answer feedback, and ordered station access.
- Simplified solid station housings and collision-safe route design.

## Acceptance rule

Island Two must read in three layers: monumental heritage landmark at distance, distinct quest laboratories at play distance, and gardens/machinery filling the perimeter without blocking approaches. No whole older assembly may be removed merely because one detail conflicts with the new style; conflicting subcomponents must be selectively retired by exact name, never by a broad wildcard that also catches architectural supports.
