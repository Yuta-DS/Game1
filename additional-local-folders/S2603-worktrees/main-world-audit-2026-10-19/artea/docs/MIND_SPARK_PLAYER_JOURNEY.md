# Mind Spark player journey

This is the canonical player-facing route for the first saved world. Each
section corresponds to an implemented quest, gate, interaction, combat beat,
reward, or return path.

## Story rule

The Blight shell is the enemy. Living creatures, feelings, thoughts, and
unfinished ideas are never enemies. The crystal sword breaks the shell and
restores what is protected inside.

## Opening — Town, Art Castle, and World Gate

Mayor Milo asks for help because Blight is spreading from the Art Castle. The
player's crystal sword can break its shell without harming the life inside.
Milo shows the Gallery, three World Gates, and parked Crown Train. The train is
optional travel for completed worlds. Violet light opens the Mind Spark gate,
and the search for the missing Star of Wisdom begins.

## Quest One — Wake the Living Network

The player follows dark server trees and glowing roots, breaks the shells around
Static Mites and Glitch Sprouts, wakes two Checkpoint Server Roots, and finds two
Thought Sparks. Each found Spark can be placed once in the Torch Spark Socket.
The socket reports `0/3`, `1/3`, and `2/3` while its beam and orb assemble.

## Quest Two — Read the Broken Pattern

At the Evidence Scanner, the player separates one fear from one fact. They
connect three large nodes on the Memory Constellation Board. Restoring the
memory awards the third Spark. Placing it completes the socket at `3/3`, forms
its crystal crown, and opens the linked Logic Gate task. The Mother Guardian may
offer a short hint but never answers for the player.

## Quest Three — Build the Hope Path

The player turns the Perspective Lens, makes the Truth Bridge solid, and frees a
Root Stalker and Blight Sentinel from their shells. Choices change the safe route
instead of grading the player. The path leads to the Thought Storm Arena.

## Quest Four — Free the Thought Storm Warden

The player dodges wide storm lines, breaks three dark anchors, and attacks the
open crystal core. Combat stops when the core reaches zero health. The final
Spark is placed in the Calm Rain Basin, changing the storm into gentle rain.

## Reward and end state

The Oldest Tree reveals the Star of Wisdom and the player receives the Wisdom
Blade. Mind Spark Station becomes an optional completed-world destination, but
the story returns through the World Gate. In the Art Castle, the Star opens the
Heart Melody World Gate.
