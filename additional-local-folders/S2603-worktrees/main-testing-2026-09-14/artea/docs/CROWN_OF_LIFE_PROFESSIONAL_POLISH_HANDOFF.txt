BASELINE VISUAL AUDIT
---------------------
Evidence inspected:
- artifacts/crown_story_01_prologue.png
- artifacts/crown_story_02_mind_spark.png

What already works:
- One continuous Castle Galaxy journey with three physically separated islands.
- Strong chapter colour coding: blue Mind, rose Heart, gold Creator.
- Milo and three guardian character models are present and readable.
- Mind sphere, Heart torus/emotion orbit, Creator canvas/choice crystals are
  mechanically distinct.
- Stars, distant castle, crown, identity star, rainbow train, HUD, captions,
  pause/settings, and final actions are present.
- Gameplay, tests, standalone PCK, and native macOS build were complete before
  this polish pass.

Professional-quality gaps to fix:
A. World surfaces
- Islands are large flat grey boxes with no top/edge/underside hierarchy.
- Walls/gates look like single translucent rectangles instead of authored
  magical architecture.
- Chasm edges and bridges lack trim, rail/light language, depth, and destination
  framing.
- Castle towers read as placeholder cylinders/cones and lack windows, doors,
  battlements, trim, or scale cues.

B. Materials / "skin"
- Most objects use one flat albedo and emission value.
- Exterior faces have no trim, secondary layer, roughness variation, or motifs.
- Black shadows are too hard; objects lose form and feel disconnected from the
  floor.
- Bright emissive props need restrained bloom/contrast instead of plastic white
  highlights.

C. Chapter identity
- Mind should feel crystalline/celestial and contemplative, not just blue.
- Heart should feel musical and alive: note paths/rings/pulses, warm floor motif,
  and six emotion colours arranged intentionally.
- Creator should feel like a luminous art studio/forge: framed canvas, palette
  accents, brush/light motifs, and a welcoming golden material language.
- These are visual refinements to existing interactions, not new mechanics.

D. Characters / staging
- Milo and guardians need grounding pools/runes/pedestals and subtle key lights.
- Scale/position should avoid props obscuring character faces.
- Labels overlap distant geometry and can be too small or visually noisy.

E. Lighting / atmosphere
- Global fill is low; shadows collapse to near-black.
- Each zone needs controlled key/fill/rim balance while keeping galaxy mood.
- Fog and star distribution need depth layers; the corridor currently ends
  abruptly in empty space.
- Avoid expensive real-time effects that break the mobile renderer.

F. Finale / composition
- The player-attached crown clips off the top of the frame and is extremely
  oversized in the existing proof.
- Rainbow train cars are floating cubes with no wheels, windows, connectors,
  engine silhouette, or luminous track/path.
- Identity star is a sphere instead of a recognisable star/sigil.
- Castle, crown, train, star, message, and buttons compete rather than forming
  one deliberate hero composition.

G. UI / rendered proof
- Small top control text has low contrast.
- Pause/settings panel is functional but visually plain and the huge world prop
  remains distracting behind it.
- Existing capture angles are diagnostic rather than presentation-quality.
- Need a new full set of polished 1280x720 renders and at least one wide world
  overview showing the complete connected journey.
