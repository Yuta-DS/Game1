# Tree of Life — 48 m Integrated Trunk and Root Architecture v3

## Reference reading

The supplied Arcane garden-tree images establish the primary biological rules:

- The trunk and roots are one continuous load-bearing mass, not stacked parts.
- Broad buttress ribbons begin at ground level, overlap, and rise into multiple
  co-dominant stems.
- The silhouette is asymmetric and gently leaning, even when the base remains
  stable and monumental.
- Pale exposed wood ridges sit over recessed charcoal-grey grooves. Colour
  changes follow growth structure rather than regular painted stripes.
- Large limbs twist as they rise; secondary limbs divide from those flows.
- Openings are formed by roots curving around negative space, never by a hard
  rectangular cut through the trunk.

The white-and-gold palace reference contributes a secondary compositional rule:
the arrival axis and central focal object should remain strongly framed. In the
tree, this becomes the south-facing walkable arch, oval heart hollow, and gold
crystal mechanism. The architecture informs hierarchy, not the tree's material.

## Diagnosis of the rejected core

- Existing authored buttresses ended near 6.05 m radius at 13–15 m height.
- The replacement core was only 4.5 m radius at ground level and tapered inward
  immediately, leaving a visible radial seam between systems.
- A straight central shell read as an inserted column rather than accumulated
  living growth.
- A rectangular heart recess repeated machine-panel language.
- Root editing collars remained visible as detached hoops.
- The root skirt and core used unrelated palettes, making even close geometry
  appear disconnected.

## Surface hierarchy

1. Charcoal cambium in the deepest grooves and heart hollow.
2. Mid-value ash wood on the continuous shell and root valleys.
3. Pale exposed wood on the twelve load-bearing ridges and doorway buttresses.
4. Warm ivory highlights on selected growth faces.
5. Cyan only in the six biological energy paths and their root tributaries.
6. Antique gold only on the heart mechanism and limited structural collars.

The garden-tree boughs remain as the biological load structure. Eleven
Christmas-tree whorls now begin at 12 m, immediately above the root flare, and
continue without a bare-trunk gap to the crown. Lower branches droop at -15°
and reach 18 m, then progressively shorten and lift until the crown tier reaches
5 m at +18°. The three lowest south-facing whorls split around the doorway and
heart sightline rather than crossing it. A single leader makes the apex read
clearly. Phase offsets, height noise and unequal radial reach prevent the
result becoming a perfectly mechanical cone. Green foliage masses overlap to
create weight. Large lanceolate leaves extend beyond those masses as curved,
solid mesh blades rather than flat four-corner cards. Every leaf carries a
raised centre-vein strip using nature-green, technology-cyan, or magic-violet
material language, and compact lilac blooms sit above and between the approved
green masses. Leaf dimensions now follow the load-bearing hierarchy: the
century-scale lower trunk carries 3× leaves, middle tiers taper to 2.6×, and the
apex uses 2.2× leaves so the crown remains pointed instead of top-heavy.

than baked into one surface:

| Secondary capillaries | 12 | subdued tributaries between the major routing systems |

Every fiber respects the doorway and heart influence zones. Ground roots use a
nonlinear thickness profile: thin at the soil tip, thickest through the root
flare, then tapered as they climb. Bark laths use unequal twist angles and small
radial noise, so their paths accumulate like real wood grain instead of forming
parallel decoration. Dark grooves sit between those laths to create readable
depth under both warm and cyan lighting.

The dark cambium web sits behind the pale structural roots and follows an
irregular six-band flare from the foundation surface to 7.35 m. Its outer edge
meets the ground at 0.035 m so the roots read as grown into the foundation, not
as tubes passing through a separate floor object. Every web face inside the
protected south gateway angle is omitted.

The heart crystal is sapphire blue with a pale-blue inner core. Four symmetric
gold prongs seat it inside the astrolabe frame, and the eight surrounding rune
jewels alternate cyan and arc-blue for an organized radial composition.

All major branches, Christmas-tier boughs, root fibers, bark laths, grooves,
capillaries, foliage clouds, leaf clusters and flower masses are separate
Blender objects. Each leaf cluster exposes nineteen distinct curved blade
islands in Edit Mode, plus raised vein strips, a non-destructive Solidify
modifier, and a small edge bevel. The machine-readable component manifest
records each object's stable name, shape family, module, material, transform
and blueprint rule.

The heart and doorway retain foliage exclusion cones. The crystal is framed by
an antique-gold ring and receives a cyan point-light accent for the Blender
renders. The canopy presentation geometry stays in the source collection. The
root and trunk sources are joined into nine runtime detail batches inside the
Web-compatible GLB while remaining independent in the editable Blender source.

Eleven authored cameras provide repeatable review images: blueprint-front and
top-down root-plan checks, a full hero view,
trunk/heart details, canopy detail, dedicated leaf-mesh close-up, root-fiber
detail, trunk-fiber detail, and gravity-bough attachment proof. Lighting uses a warm directional key, cool
canopy fill, dark green garden world, and AgX contrast.

The same Godot material instances are applied to the Blender core and existing
root skirt at runtime, preventing sanctuary lighting from creating a new colour
boundary. Editing collars are retained in Blender but hidden in game.

## Implementation constraints

- Blender source remains fully editable, includes the presentation canopy, and
  exports one compact 48 m trunk/root GLB with nine batched detail systems for Godot.
- The production Earth/Art Castle tree instantiates that GLB as
  `BlenderBlueprintTrunkRoot` at 3.875× scale: 48 m becomes the 186 m world body
  and the 39.2 m root diameter becomes the 151.9 m world root spread.
- The Blender body replaces only the coarse procedural lower roots, doorway,
  buttresses, and their duplicate energy network. The established upper leader,
  gravity-bough canopy, socket leaves, heart shrine, and crown star remain live.
- The production body contributes 58,904 triangles once. Its shadow casting is
  disabled because the canopy owns the landmark shadow, preserving laptop GPU time.
- No runtime booleans, skeleton, displacement, or texture dependency.
- Godot uses simple collision proxies and preserves the walkable south arch.
- The leaf and branch projection-clear niche remains active around the crystal.
- The design is compatible with the non-Mono Godot Web renderer.

## Acceptance

- No visible air gap or material seam between authored roots and core.
- Root ridges visually continue into the core from frontal and three-quarter
  views.
- The crystal reads inside an organic hollow, not on a rectangular panel.
- The doorway remains traversable by the production player.
- Foliage does not cover the heart focal area.
- Web triangle and draw complexity stay within the established budget.
